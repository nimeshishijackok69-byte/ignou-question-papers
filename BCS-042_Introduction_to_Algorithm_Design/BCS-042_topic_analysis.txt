WHAT CHANGED vs THE 2021-2024 ANALYSIS
--------------------------------------
 1. MST dipped for the first time ever in Dec 2024, then came back
    strong - and June 2026 raised the stakes by asking Kruskal AND
    Prim together (8 marks).
 2. Bubble sort overtook quick/merge sort as the standard trace
    question; selection sort is a newcomer (Dec24, Dec25).
 3. DFS replaced BFS as the default traversal question since 2024.
 4. Dijkstra questions now use DIRECTED graphs (all 3 of Dec24/Jun25/Dec25).
 5. Fractional knapsack + greedy have become near-regulars (3 of last 4).
 6. Literal question repeats: GCD(595,252) in Dec25 AND Jun26;
    T(n)=2T(n/3)+n recursion-tree in Jun25 AND Jun26;
    the same induction proof in Jun23, Jun24, Jun26.
    => IGNOU recycles whole questions across consecutive sessions.

EXAM PATTERN (still holds, 11 papers)
