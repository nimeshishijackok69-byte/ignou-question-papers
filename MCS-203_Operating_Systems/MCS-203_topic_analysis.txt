STRUCTURE FACTS (all 7 papers)
------------------------------
 - Q1 is ALWAYS 4 sub-parts of 10 marks each (Dec-2024 exception:
   5 parts = 10+5+5+10+10). Same total: 40.
 - Q2-Q4: two 10-mark halves each (one paper used 8+8+4).

   exactly 4, no choice).

   fragments. Q1 tests one full topic per part, not 9 micro-topics.

    - Producer-Consumer with semaphores: Jun23 Q1(a), Dec24 Q1(e).
    - Dining Philosophers with semaphores: Jun25 Q1(c), Dec25 Q1(b).

Q1 COMPULSORY - PREDICTABLE SHAPE (all 7 papers)
------------------------------------------------
Four 10-mark parts drawn from a fixed menu (Dec-24 used 5 smaller parts):
  (1) ONE numerical: CPU-scheduling Gantt / disk head movement /
      page-fault count (present in 6/7 papers - Jun24 the exception)
  (2) ONE synchronization question: semaphore + classical problem
      (5/7) or deadlock RAG (2/7)
  (3) ONE memory-management question: paging / segmentation /
      virtual memory
  (4) ONE "product or system" theory part: LINUX arch, iOS layers,
      Windows process mgmt, distributed design issues, OS types,
      Lamport's ME
=> If you own the numerical recipes + 3 classical sync problems +
   paging/segmentation/VM cards, Q1 is 30+ of 40 before you open
   the product cards.

MARKS RATIO (by marks offered, 7-paper average)
-----------------------------------------------
 ~20% pure numerical (CPU scheduling, disk head movement, page-fault
     counting, first/best/worst fit, RAG cycle analysis)
 ~30-35% diagram + explain (LINUX arch, iOS/Android layers, paging/
     segmentation mapping, DSM, DMA-free but diagram-heavy)
 ~45-50% pure theory (algorithms explained, conditions, features,
     comparisons, design issues)
NOTE: This is the LEAST numerical of your MCS courses - the mirror
image of MCS-202 (~40% numerical). BUT the numericals that do appear
are 100% template-based: only 4 recipes exist (Gantt-chart scheduling,
disk head movement, page-fault table, fit algorithms) and each has
appeared in 3-4 of 7 papers, always 10-30 marks. Zero derivation,
zero math beyond counting. Theory is where marks live here - and it
is delivered in fixed 10-mark answer-card format.
